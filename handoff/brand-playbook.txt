2. Message Pillars:

Pillar 1 -- Local & Accessible
Melbourne's west has been underserved. Resonant Studios is here -- in the community, not across
the city. Physical accessibility and sensory-friendly design are built in from day one, not added as
a n afterthought.

Pillar 2 -- Professional Output Quality
Every participant finishes with a real, professionally produced song on Spotify. Not a participation
certificate. Not a recording on a phone. A song. Industry-grade production meets
disability-informed practice.

Pillar 3 -- Lived Experience & Cultural Competency
Antony's lived experience with disability is not a line on a resume -- it is the reason this studio
exists. Participants and referrers can trust that the environment is genuinely safe, genuinely
adaptive, and genuinely informed.

Pillar 4 -- Participant-Led Customisation
This is not a course. There is no fixed curriculum. Each participant leads -- Antony produces.
Sessions adapt to pace, mood, goals, and ability. The journey is theirs.
