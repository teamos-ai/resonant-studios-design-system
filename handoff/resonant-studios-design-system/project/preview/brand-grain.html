With grain · 22%
Grain lives in a fixed, pointer-events: none pseudo-element on body. Don’t skip it — this is what makes surfaces feel like paper, not plastic. Dial per surface: 22% multiply on copper-deep, 18% multiply on sage-deep, 16% screen on black (screen mode lifts the texture instead of darkening it), and 6–9% multiply on paper/oat. Tile at 220px — large enough to read as fibre, small enough to avoid visible repetition at card sizes.
