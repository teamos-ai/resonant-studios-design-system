# Impeccable — project context

> Design context for the Resonant Studios design system. Derived from `BRIEF.md` (the canonical source of truth) on 2026-05-02. If anything here conflicts with `BRIEF.md`, the BRIEF wins — update this file.

## Design Context

### Users

Two registers, one system. Both run through the same components — variant via copy, not via separate components.

- **Participants** (and families/carers) — adults with disability who self-manage or are plan-managed under the NDIS. Reading age targeted at Year 7 max (Plain English Australia). Many are using the site at home, sometimes with carer support, often on phones. Some have low vision, dyslexia, motor differences, or anxiety. Surface: Home, For Participants, Stories, Discovery call form. Register: warm, affirming, informal — *"Your song. Your story. Your way."*
- **Support coordinators + plan managers** — NDIS professionals who refer participants and approve invoices. They scan for outcomes, line items, and plan-goal alignment. Surface: For Support Coordinators, FAQ, intake docs. Register: professional, structured, NDIS-literate.

The product itself is a 12-week music-production program at a boutique studio in Werribee/Wyndham (Melbourne's west). Each participant works toward a finished song released on Spotify under their own name. Antony Rako (founder) brings 25+ years of professional music production and lived disability experience.

### Brand Personality

Three concrete words: **warm, crafted, calm**. Plus *accessible · professional · human · premium but not exclusive*.

Tone-of-voice in one line: a structured support service delivered by musicians. Warm but clear. Outcome-focused. Never sales-y. Never framed as studio hire. Never clinical.

Master tagline: *Your song. Your story. On Spotify.*

**Banned vocabulary** (enforced as component contracts, not just guidance):
- Hospital / clinical: `patient`, `treatment`, `therapy`, `therapist`, `clinical`, `clinician`, `intervention`, `modality`, `diagnosis`, `disorder`, `condition`, `disability services`
- Industry / commodity: `music therapy`, `studio hire`, `music production services`, `clients`, `users`
- Sales: `Get Started`, `Sign Up Now`, `Limited Time`, `Don't Miss Out`, trailing `!`

**Approved core terms:** `participant`, `you`, `your song`, `your story`, `discovery call`, `session`, `support coordinator`, `plan manager`, `self-managed`, `plan-managed`, `studio`, `track`/`song`.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Direction B — "Field Recording"**, dual-mode, dark default.

- **Theme**: dual-mode with dark as default (accessibility-led — many participants browse late at night on phones; halation from pure black is forbidden). Manual `data-theme` overrides OS preference.
- **Palette**: warm earthy. Terracotta (`#C76F46` light / `#E58F66` dark) is the brand identity colour — eyebrows, focus rings, links, logo, badges. Sage forest (`#728C73` / `#9BB89C`) carries the primary CTA load (terracotta felt too aggressive for the audience). Slate (`#4A4F5C` / `#8C95A8`) is the cool/professional accent. Pure `#000` is **forbidden**.
- **Typography**: two faces only. **DM Serif Display** for H1–H3, pull quotes, song titles (italic cuts are signature). **Manrope** for body/UI/buttons/labels (tabular-nums for codes/hex). **Atkinson Hyperlegible** opt-in via accessibility menu.
- **Shape language**: rounded squares, not pills. `--r-2` (10px) for buttons/inputs/badges, `--r-3` (14px) for cards, up to `--r-5` (28px) for hero cards. `--r-pill` (999px) reserved for genuinely circular elements only — avatars, switch tracks, status dots, audio play buttons.
- **Motion**: `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` (out-expo) only. No springs, no bounces. Durations: 140ms micro · 220ms component · 420ms scene. Entrance fades + 4–8px Y-rise; exit is fade only. Press is `scale(0.98)` on buttons only. Forbidden: parallax, auto-rotating carousels, auto-playing video, loops > 5s, animated GIFs.
- **Imagery**: real studio detail — Antony's gear (keyboard, drum pads, condenser mic), hands on instruments, textured walls. Warm, natural, slightly desaturated. **Forbidden**: stock smiling-headshot photography, neon producer studios, clinical hospital imagery, high-fives, processed HDR.
- **Iconography**: Lucide icons, **1.5px stroke** (not the 2px default), `currentColor` only. Never multi-coloured. Never filled. 16px floor, 20px inline body, 24px nav, 32–40px feature grids.
- **Anti-references** (what this should explicitly NOT look like): generic NDIS provider sites with stock smiling photos and corporate blue gradients · clinical/hospital portals · neon producer-studio aesthetics · Bootstrap-y SaaS landing pages · the "AI design tells" gallery (gradient text, side-stripe cards, glassmorphism, hero metric blocks, cyan-on-dark, purple-to-blue gradients).

### Design Principles

1. **Accessibility is a foundation, not a feature.** WCAG 2.2 AA minimum across all interactive surfaces; AAA body contrast (7:1) for long-form. 48×48px minimum touch targets. Focus rings always visible — `outline: none` is forbidden. `prefers-reduced-motion`, `prefers-contrast: more`, `prefers-reduced-data` are all honoured. User-toggleable easy-read mode and dyslexia-friendly font are first-class, not afterthoughts.
2. **Token discipline is the contract.** Three tiers: primitive (raw), semantic (role), component (per-component, reserved). No hex codes in component files, ever. No primitive tokens in component files. Mode-switching = swap tier-2 values; never edit components. Inline `style={{}}` must reference tier-2 only.
3. **Voice is a component contract, not guidance.** Banned-words lists are linted. Microcopy contracts are explicit (button = verb-phrase sentence-case no `!`; field error = plain language, never blame, tells user *what to do*; loading = `"Loading…"`/`"One moment."` never `"Hold tight!"`). Plain English Australia thresholds: Year 7 reading age, sentence length avg ≤18 words, active voice by default, Saxon over Latinate.
4. **Calm over flashy.** No autoplay anything. No parallax. No bounces. Premium-but-not-exclusive — the studio is for participants, not for showing off. Music-industry "producer aesthetic" is explicitly rejected.
5. **One system, two registers.** Same components, different copy. The system must be legible to both an anxious 19-year-old participant and a time-pressured support coordinator scanning for line items.

## Locked technical floor (from BRIEF.md §5, §5b, §6, §7)

These are not preferences — they are commitments. Audit findings should grade against them.

- WCAG 2.2 AA across interactive surfaces; AAA body contrast for long-form (7:1)
- 48×48px touch targets (Material spec, exceeds Apple 44×44)
- Focus rings: 2px primary outline + 2px offset, always visible
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: transforms collapse, only opacity transitions remain
- `prefers-contrast: more`: borders thicken, shadows strengthen
- `prefers-reduced-data`: heavy hero imagery becomes solid colour
- One `h1` per page, no skipped levels (enforced via `<Heading>` component)
- `lang="en-AU"`
- Skip link first-focusable on every page
- Visible labels always; never placeholder-as-label; errors with `role="alert"`
- Audio: never autoplay; transcript link mandatory; captions for any video
- Heading-and-button microcopy contracts (sentence case, verb phrases, no `!`, banned-words list, FK Grade ≤ 7)
- Easing: out-expo only; durations 140/220/420ms; no springs, no bounces
- Shape: rounded squares default; `--r-pill` reserved for circular intent only
